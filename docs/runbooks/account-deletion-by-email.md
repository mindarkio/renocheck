# Runbook: delete an account from an email request

While quote analysis or login is closed, the disclaimer and the account page tell people to email hello@renocheck.homes to delete an account. Aaron accepted this procedure on 2026-10-24 (DEC-0013).

## When to act

Act only on a request sent from the account's registered email address.

## Before deleting

Reply to that registered address and wait for confirmation from it before deleting.
